The Business Problem

In institutional trading, settlements do not happen instantly. When a counterparty delays their payment, it ties up internal capital and introduces Liquidity Risk. Previously, the finance team tracked these delays manually using massive Excel spreadsheets, making it impossible to catch high-exposure delays in real-time before they became a financial liability.

Figure 1: Financial risk exposure monitoring and data architecture.

Methodology & Architecture

  • Data Extraction: Developed complex SQL queries within the Snowflake data warehouse to pull and aggregate historical trade lifecycle data, calculating exact settlement delays by comparing expected vs. actual dates.
  • Statistical Anomaly Detection: Fed the SQL output into a Python script. Utilized Pandas and NumPy to calculate the Mean and Standard Deviation of delays by Asset Class, applying a Z-Score methodology to automatically flag statistically significant outliers.
  • Executive Dashboarding: Designed a Power BI dashboard to visualize the findings, allowing stakeholders to see a ranked list of "Critical Risk" counterparties instantly.

The Impact

By shifting from manual Excel reports to an automated SQL/Python pipeline, the business realized a 34% reduction in manual reporting hours. Furthermore, the statistical anomaly detection engine improved early identification of liquidity stress indicators by 17%, allowing executive leadership to renegotiate terms with consistently late counterparties.
