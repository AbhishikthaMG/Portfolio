Institutional Portfolio Liquidity Risk Analytics

Automating Risk Detection Across 800K+ Trades

Abhishiktha Munaswamy Gurappa | Data Analyst

1. The Business Problem

  • Delayed Settlements: Institutional trades do not settle instantly. When counterparties delay payment, it creates Liquidity Risk by tying up our capital.
  • Scale & Visibility: Manual tracking of 800,000+ trades in Excel made it impossible to catch high-exposure delays in real-time.
  • The Goal: Automatically flag these delays before they become a massive financial risk to the portfolio.

2. End-to-End Analytics Pipeline

  • Storage: Snowflake Data Warehouse
  • Querying & Aggregation: Complex SQL joins to calculate time-difference metrics across counterparties.
  • Anomaly Detection: Python (Pandas & NumPy) statistical engine.
  • Visualization: Power BI Executive Dashboard for daily monitoring.

3. Flagging Risk with Python

  • Calculated the Mean and Standard Deviation of settlement delays segmented by Asset Class.
  • Applied Z-Score methodology to detect statistically significant outliers.
  • Automatically categorized massive datasets into Normal, Elevated Risk, and Critical Risk buckets based on their exposure amount and delay length.

4. Business Value & Impact

+17% Risk Detection

Significantly improved early identification of liquidity stress indicators, protecting firm capital.

-34% Manual Effort

Eliminated the need for weekly manual Excel reporting pipelines, saving hours of analyst time.

Proactive Action

Allowed executive leadership to renegotiate trading terms with counterparties who were consistently flagged as high-risk.
